The Story

Raised on the fish pier, line by line.

Taylor Brown grew up on the docks of the Chatham Fish Pier. Her father, Nick Brown, fished the waters off Chatham commercially for more than twenty-five years — and Taylor grew up sketching the gear, the boats, and the water while the catch came in.

Those hand-drawn lines became Fisherman's Daughter. Taylor began drawing original ocean-inspired graphics and hand screen-printing them onto tee shirts; today it's a full collection for women, men, kids, and infants — every design still drawn by hand, every piece still printed on Cape Cod.

"Every print starts the way the day starts on the pier — with a line in the water."

— Taylor Brown, founder & illustrator
