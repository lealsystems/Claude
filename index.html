Chimney & Brick Masonry

Thirty courses up a scaffold or one cracked joint at the firebox, masonry is where Tim started and where his hand shows most. Crowns rebuilt to shed water, repointing matched to the original mortar, fireboxes and surrounds laid true, and full chimney rebuilds that stand plumb against a Cape nor'easter.
