What We Build

Premium Materials, Master Execution

Every project starts with the best material for a salt-air climate and ends with detail work you can run your hand across.

01

Premium Decking

Outdoor living built for the Cape — composite and hardwood decks in premium, low-maintenance materials engineered to shrug off salt, sun, and nor'easters. Framing, railings, and stairs finished square and true.

02

Premium Siding

Classic Cape Cod exteriors — red cedar shingles, clapboard, and premium engineered siding installed with proper flashing and trim details, so the weathered-gray look stays beautiful and watertight for decades.

The last five percent that makes the whole project — custom built-ins, wainscoting, crown, casing, mantels, and stair work. Tight miters, scribed fits, and furniture-grade finishes by hand.
