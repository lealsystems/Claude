# SA Construction Inc — Logo

A minimalist redesign of the original badge logo. All files are scalable SVG.

## Concept

The original mark (circular saw blade + house + arched text) is reduced to its
essential idea — **precision cutting and homebuilding** — using a single
consistent line weight and generous negative space:

- **The circle** is the blade, drawn as two thin arcs.
- **The horizontal line** is the kerf — the cut passing clean through the blade,
  doubling as the ground line the house sits on.
- **The segmented lower arc** reads as the rim of a diamond saw blade.
- **The chevron + dot** form the house: roofline and door, nothing more.
- Arched text is replaced with letterspaced geometric capitals.
