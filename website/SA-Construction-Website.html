The Company

We partner with remarkable clients and their architects to bring exceptional design to life — in solid oak, joined by hand, finished to a standard that machines alone can’t reach.

SA Construction is a general contractor with a cabinetmaker’s temperament. Our reputation is built on architectural paneling and casework: quarter-sawn and rift-sawn white oak, book-matched veneers, hand-beveled coffers, and joinery that closes with the sound of a bank-vault door. We run lean crews, communicate plainly, and treat every home as if our own name were carved into the newel post.

From a single paneled study to a whole-home renovation, the same rule applies: honest materials, precise measurement, and finishes that ask to be touched. We shop-fabricate, dry-fit, and install white-glove — so the dust stays in our shop and the craftsmanship stays in your walls.

Selected Work
Our Material

White oak, three ways. The same tree, sawn three directions, gives three different rooms.

Quarter‑Sawn

Cut across the growth rings for ray fleck and figure. Stable, traditional, and luminous under a hand-rubbed oil finish. Our choice for libraries and paneled rooms.

Rift‑Sawn

Straight, calm, linear grain with no cathedral. The modern workhorse — kitchens, slab doors, and long runs where the eye should rest, not wander.

Fumed

Darkened to the core in an ammonia chamber — never stained. Deep, smoky tones that wear beautifully in lounges, bars, and wine rooms.
