What We Craft

One team, from first sketch to final coat

Interior designers, master carpenters, and project managers under one roof — so your project never loses momentum between trades.

Custom Wine Cellars

Climate-smart, gallery-grade cellars — racking, arches, and lighting tailored to your collection and your home's architecture.

Kitchen Cabinetry

Fully bespoke kitchen cabinets — inset doors, soft-close everything, and finishes matched to your space, not a catalog.

Built-Ins & Millwork

Window seats, libraries, media walls, and mudroom systems — built to the inch and finished like furniture.

Coffered Ceilings

Dimensional ceilings that transform flat rooms into architecture — beams, panels, and crown detailed in 3D first.

Stairs & Railings

Statement staircases, balustrades, and newel posts — the first thing guests see, built to be the best thing they see.

Trim & Wainscoting

Crown molding, picture-frame wainscoting, shiplap, and casings — the finish work that separates good rooms from great ones.

Walk-In Closets

Boutique-style dressing rooms with islands, glass fronts, and integrated lighting — organized down to the last drawer.

Renovations & Additions

Whole-room transformations and additions, managed end-to-end by one accountable team from permit to punch list.

3D Design & Rendering

Walk through your project before a single board is cut — photoreal renderings that eliminate guesswork and surprises.

About Capefort

Design minds and maker's hands, under one roof

Capefort Custom Carpentry is a Cape Cod luxury design-and-build company that turns visions into reality with precision and care. Our team of interior designers, skilled construction professionals, and supportive staff guides every project from conception to completion.

Because design and craftsmanship live under the same roof, nothing is lost in translation between the drawing and the finished piece — the 3D rendering you approve is the room you get.

  • ✓One accountable team — design, build, and install with no hand-offs.
  • ✓Furniture-grade standards — premium hardwoods, precise joinery, flawless finish.
  • ✓Clear communication — proactive updates, honest timelines, tidy job sites.
